Contact

The quickest way: in the app, open About (ⓘ) → Send Feedback, or Report a Problem at the bottom of a restaurant's page.

Common questions

A menu or price is wrong, or a restaurant has closed.
Open the restaurant in Dishbase and tap Report a Problem. Menus are read from restaurants' own websites and are refreshed regularly.

How do I keep my ratings if I get a new phone?
Use About → Export My Ratings to save a file, then Import Ratings on the new phone. When iCloud is on for Dishbase, your ratings also come back automatically with the same Apple account.

Why does Dishbase ask for my location?
Only to show the nearest restaurants first and to filter by distance. It stays on your phone. You can say no and still use everything else.

A restaurant is missing.
Dishbase covers restaurants in Wake County, NC, from the county's open data. Tell us through Send Feedback.

How do I delete my data?
Swipe left on a rating in My Dishes to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything on the phone (iCloud data can be removed in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Dishbase).
