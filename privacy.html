Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

In short: Dishbase has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email or other personal information. Your ratings, notes, dish photos and location stay on your phone (and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud). The only photos we receive are menu photos you choose to upload; they're deleted once the menu is read. We don't track you, show ads, or sell or share data.

Your ratings, notes and photos

Everything you record about dishes is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for Dishbase, it's also kept in your private iCloud database so it survives a reinstall or a new phone. Apple stores that data for you; we (the developer) can't see it. You can delete any rating in the app, export your ratings to a file you choose, or delete everything by deleting the app (and its iCloud data in iPhone Settings).

Your location

If you allow it, Dishbase uses your location only on your phone to sort restaurants by distance and to filter by miles. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Dishbase.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a photo of a dish or upload photos of a menu. Dish photos stay with your ratings on your phone (and your private iCloud, if on). Menu photos are sent to us, as described below.

Menu photos you upload

If you use Upload a Menu on a restaurant page, the app sends only: the photos you picked (made smaller on your phone), the restaurant they're for, the number of pages, and the app version. No name, email, account, device ID or location is attached. We use the photos only to read the menu's dishes and prices, with the help of an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) that processes them for that purpose; every menu is reviewed by a person before it appears in the app. The photos are deleted once the menu has been read; the dishes and prices from the menu may be shown to all Dishbase users. Please photograph only the menu, not people or anything personal.

Restaurant and menu information

To show restaurants and menus, the app downloads them from our database (hosted by Supabase in the United States). These requests don't include anything about you. Like any internet service, our host briefly records technical information needed to deliver the data, such as the internet address a request came from; we don't use it to identify you.

Feedback and problem reports

If you use Report a Problem or Send Feedback, the app sends only: the restaurant concerned (for a report), the reason you picked, your optional comment, and the app version. No name, email, account, device ID or location is attached. Please don't write personal information in the comment. Reports are used only to fix restaurant information and improve the app.

Apple

Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers only if you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings. If you test Dishbase through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send, along with your TestFlight details.

Children

Dishbase is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Data sources

Restaurant names and addresses come from Wake County, NC open data (CC BY 4.0). Menus are read from restaurants' own websites. Map and restaurant details partly come from © OpenStreetMap contributors and Overture Maps Foundation.

Changes and contact
